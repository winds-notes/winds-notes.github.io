Notebooks & pages

Create your first notebook
  1. On the library screen, choose New notebook or Create a notebook.
  2. Name it, pick a cover, choose ruled, grid, dotted, or blank paper, and set portrait or landscape orientation.
  3. Create the notebook, then choose Pen and start writing. The status near the notebook title shows when your work is saved on this device.
Find, rename, or delete a notebook
  • Use Search notebooks and the sort menu in the library to find a title. Continue reopens your last notebook and page.
  • Rename a notebook by editing its title in the editor or using its three-dot menu in the library.
  • Use the library’s three-dot menu or Notebook options → Delete notebook to delete. For shared notebooks, Remove from my library removes your library entry.
Add, scroll, duplicate, and delete pages
  • Tap + to add a page. Use the page arrows or open Page thumbnails to jump between pages.
  • Scroll with a finger in Stylus only mode, or choose the hand tool (Scroll pages) and drag.
  • Choose Notebook options → Duplicate current page for an editable copy. Use Delete beside a page heading or Delete current page. A notebook keeps at least one page.
  • Pinch with two fingers, use the zoom buttons, or tap the zoom percentage for presets. Choose Fit page to width in Notebook options to reset the view.
Change paper, orientation, and theme
  • Use Paper template and Page orientation in the toolbar to change the current notebook page. Imported PDF pages keep their original background and dimensions.
  • Choose Theme in the library or Notebook options, then pick Match device, Light, or Dark for the app interface.
Save a personal page template
  1. Set up a page you want to reuse, such as a lab worksheet, planner, or diagram outline.
  2. Open Notebook options → Page templates, enter a name, and choose Save template.
  3. Open the same menu in any notebook in your workspace. Select a template and choose Add as new page to insert an editable copy.

Your template library is stored for the current account on this device.

Handwriting & brushes

Write with Pen and Highlighter
  1. Choose Pen for handwriting or Highlighter for translucent marks.
  2. Pick an ink swatch or open More ink colors. Drag the width slider to set thickness.
  3. With Stylus only enabled, Apple Pencil draws and fingers scroll or pinch. Turn it off to draw with a finger. A mouse can also draw.

Swipe the toolbar or use its edge arrows to reach more controls on a narrow screen.

Draw with Artist Studio brushes
  1. Tap Artist to browse or search Graphite, Ink, Pigment, Paint, and Effects.
  2. Choose a material such as HB, Charcoal, Calligraphy Nib, or Watercolor Wash. Adjust Size and Flow using the brush preview.
  3. Draw on the page. Tap Pen to return to regular handwriting.
Erase part of an object
  1. Tap Eraser and brush across the part of the ink, text, sticker, shape, or image you want to remove.
  2. Use the arrow next to Eraser to adjust the diameter or choose Ink only to protect other objects. The app remembers your eraser size.
  3. Use Undo or Redo to step through edits. An imported PDF background stays fixed when you erase annotations.
Check Apple Pencil input
  1. Open Notebook options → Apple Pencil input test.
  2. Try several short strokes in a row and a long continuous stroke while checking the input panel. Close the panel when you finish.

Text, images & editing

Select, move, and edit handwriting or objects
  • Choose Select and tap an item, then drag it to move it.
  • Choose Area select handwriting and draw a box around several strokes to move, copy, or cut them together.
  • Use the toolbar width slider after selecting a stroke, shape, vector path, or graph to change its line thickness.
  • Use Delete selection in Notebook options to remove the selected item or group.
Type and format text
  1. Choose Text and tap the page, or paste text from another app.
  2. Choose Select, tap the text box, and open Notebook options → Edit selected text.
  3. Edit the text and adjust font, size, color, emphasis, alignment, line spacing, or box width. Choose Apply changes.
Copy, cut, and paste
  • Select an item or handwriting group and choose Copy or Cut. Use Notebook options → Paste copied object to place an editable copy.
  • Use your system’s paste command to bring in text or an image. On touch devices, Notebook options → Paste opens a box where you can paste text using the device’s menu.
Insert, resize, rotate, and crop images
  1. Choose Insert an image and pick a file. Select the image and drag a corner to resize it, or enter a width under Notebook options → Size and angle.
  2. Drag the round rotation handle, or enter an angle in Size and angle. Hold Shift while rotating to snap to 15° increments.
  3. Tap the crop handle beside the selected image. Drag the crop edges, move the frame, or use Exact crop. Choose Apply crop. Reopen cropping and choose Reset to restore the full image.

Corner resize and rotation also work on stickers and supported shapes.

Shapes, stickers & graphs

Draw and style shapes, lines, and arrows
  1. Open the arrow beside Shapes, choose a figure, then drag on the page to draw it.
  2. Use Fill settings for fill color and opacity, outline color and width, and solid, dashed, dotted, or hidden outlines.
  3. To restyle an existing shape, select it and reopen Fill settings. Line and Arrow use outline settings.
Draw stretchable math symbols
  1. Choose Shapes → Math symbols, pick a brace, bracket, root, fraction bar, or other symbol, and drag to place it.
  2. Choose Select and drag the corner handles, or enter exact width and height under Size and angle.
  3. Drag the toolbar width slider to adjust the line thickness of the selected symbol.
Fill a hand-drawn area or sticker region
  1. Draw a closed outline or insert an illustrated sticker. Choose Fill and pick a color from the toolbar or the adjacent color chip.
  2. Tap inside the outline or on a colored sticker region. Keep tapping to fill more regions or switch colors while Fill is active.
  3. If a hand-drawn area won’t fill, close any gap in its outline. Choose another tool or press Esc when you’re finished.
Insert and edit stickers
  1. Tap the smiley button or choose Notebook options → Stickers.
  2. Browse Illustrated, Everyday, Education, Science, Art, or Engineering. Use search to find a design.
  3. Set the sticker size and tap a design to insert it. Use Select to move, resize, rotate, or copy it. Illustrated stickers stay sharp when enlarged.
Add a mandala coloring page
  1. Open the arrow beside Fill and choose Mandala coloring pages.
  2. Browse or search the designs, select one, and choose Add coloring page.
  3. Use Fill to color the enclosed sections. Use Undo to try another color.
Draw a precise vector path
  1. Choose Shapes → Draw vector path. Tap to add anchors. Drag from a new anchor to create a Bézier curve.
  2. Adjust the handles, enter exact X/Y coordinates, or enable Snap to grid. Close the path to add a fill.
  3. Insert the path, then select it to reopen Edit path or choose Export → Selected vector path to save an SVG.
Insert an interactive graph
  1. Choose Notebook options → Insert graph. Enter y = ax², set the axis ranges and parameter range, then insert the graph.
  2. Select the graph and move the a slider to change the curve, or reopen its equation editor.
  3. Use Pen to annotate over the graph. An editable notebook keeps the equation and parameter. PDF and image exports capture the current curve.

Study & presentation

Import and annotate a PDF
  1. Choose Import PDF in the library or Notebook options → Import PDF pages. Enter the password if prompted.
  2. Select page numbers or ranges such as 1-5, 8. Create a new notebook or append the selected pages to the open notebook.
  3. Write, highlight, and draw on top of the PDF pages. Export an annotated PDF to share or an editable notebook to keep working.

You can select up to 50 pages per import from a PDF under 25 MB. Imported pages are rendered as page images.

Study beside a PDF and insert a figure
  1. Open a notebook and choose Notebook options → Study beside a PDF. Pick an imported PDF notebook or choose Import a PDF.
  2. Use PDF and Notes zoom separately. Fit fits the corresponding page to its pane. Drag the divider to give one side more room. On narrow screens the panes are stacked, so drag up or down.
  3. Use the reference pane’s page controls to find a figure. Tap Select figure and drag a box around it.
  4. Drag the selected area onto your writing page or tap Insert figure. Select the inserted image and choose Open linked page to return to the source.
Link handwriting, labels, and diagrams to pages
  1. Select an object, then choose Notebook options → Link selection to a page.
  2. Choose a notebook and page, then tap Save link. Reopen the same menu to edit or remove the link.
  3. Use Open linked page on the selected object, or turn on Follow links on the page from Notebook options and tap linked objects directly. Choose Pen to resume writing.
Present a notebook with a laser pointer
  1. Choose Notebook options → Present notebook, then choose Present here or Open projector window. Move the separate window to your second display when available.
  2. Use Start with blank page to hide page objects, then Reveal next or the reveal slider to show them in layer order. Page backgrounds remain visible.
  3. Point on the preview or presentation surface to show a temporary laser dot. Use the page controls to advance and End presentation when finished.

Reopen an editable file or restore a backup
  1. Choose Open notebook in the library or Notebook options → Open notebook file.
  2. Pick an editable .winds-notes or compatible .json file. Imports are added to the library as copies.
  3. Use Notebook options → Back up all notebooks for a workspace backup, and Restore notebook backup to open one later.
Work offline or without a Google account

Open the app online once and let the offline files finish installing. After that, you can write, draw, and use this guide from the same browser while offline. Guest notebooks are saved separately on this device. Keep an editable backup if you plan to clear browser data or move devices.

Google Drive & sharing

Connect Google Drive and sync across devices
  1. Choose Sign in with Google in the library, or open Google Drive from Notebook options. Use the same account on each device for your personal notebooks.
  2. Keep Sync automatically when idle checked. Use Sync notebooks now for a manual check.
  3. Expand Sync details to see the last upload and received change. A site configured for live previews can show some edits sooner. Drive uploads save the changes remotely.

Use Switch Google account to open a different workspace or Sign out / open guest notes to return to guest notes. Google may ask you to reconnect when its authorization expires.

Compress Drive uploads

Open Google Drive and enable Compress Drive workspace uploads when available to reduce upload size. Use an up-to-date copy of WiNDS Notes on the devices that sync this workspace.

Create or import a dated Drive backup
  1. Open Google Drive and choose Create dated Drive backup for a snapshot.
  2. Choose Import an earlier Drive backup and select the snapshot you want to reopen.
Share a notebook and open an invitation
  1. Sign in with Google. In My notebooks, open a notebook’s three-dot menu and choose Share notebook.
  2. Enter the collaborator’s Google email and choose Share and send invitation.
  3. The recipient signs in with the invited account and uses the invitation link, Open shared link, or Open shared from Drive.
  4. If prompted, use Check access in Google Drive, then Authorize this notebook and select its shared folder. If Drive cannot open the folder, ask the owner to check the invitation and edit permission.
